# Darwin Cast for Android

Cast local movies, public YouTube videos and detected website streams to the Darwin receiver on a supported Samsung TV. The phone serves the video over the local network and must stay powered and connected while casting.

Darwin 0.4.0 includes saved English/Mexican Spanish settings, a portrait QR scanner, and a clear TV volume control.

Darwin includes a library, queue, remote, playback timeline, audio/subtitle choices, saved subtitle appearance, and playback diagnostics. Native phone casting and imported subtitles work without a Mac. OpenSubtitles search and the TV web-browser tools use the optional local Mac controller.

This is preview software. DRM, sign-in-only YouTube, expired links, provider restrictions and some players/codecs remain unsupported. Live streams may not be seekable. High-resolution software conversion depends on the phone's performance; very large files are range-streamed rather than loaded into memory.
